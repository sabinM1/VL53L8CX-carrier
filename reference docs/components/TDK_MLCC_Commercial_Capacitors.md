# TDK MLCC — Commercial Grade General Capacitors (Up to 75 V), C Series

Source file: `mlcc_commercial_general_en.pdf`

## Series Overview
General-purpose surface-mount multilayer ceramic chip capacitors. Monolithic structure gives high mechanical strength, high reliability, low ESR, and low ESL. Capacitance range up to 220 µF.

## Features
- Superior mechanical strength and high reliability
- Outstanding frequency characteristics: low ESR, low ESL
- Low self-heating and high ripple resistance due to low ESR
- No polarity

## Applications
- General electronic equipment
- Mobile devices
- Servers, PCs, tablets
- Power supply circuits

## Capacitance Range Summary

- X7R: 100 pF to 47 µF
