# Yageo X5R MLCC — Surface Mount Multilayer Ceramic Capacitors, 4 V to 50 V

Source file: `upy-gphc_x5r_4v-to-50v.pdf`

## Scope
X5R series chip capacitors with lead-free terminations.

## Applications
- PCs, hard disk, game PCs
- Power supplies
- DVD players
- Mobile phones
- Data processing

## Features
- Supplied in tape on reel
- Nickel-barrier end termination
- RoHS compliant
- Halogen free

## Ordering Information — Global Part Number
Format:
`CC XXXX X X X5R X BB XXX`

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| (1) Size | 0201 (0603), 0402 (1005), 0603 (1608), 0805 (2012), 1206 (3216), 1210 (3225) |
| (2) Tolerance | K = ±10%, M = ±20% |
| (3) Packing | R = paper/PE 7-in reel; K = blister 7-in; P = paper/PE 13-in; F = blister 13-in; C = bulk |
| (4) Rated voltage | 4 = 4 V; 5 = 6.3 V; 6 = 10 V; 7 = 16 V; 8 = 25 V; 9 = 50 V |
| (5) Capacitance | 2 significant digits + zeros; R = decimal point; e.g. 103 = 10 nF |

## Construction
Rectangular ceramic dielectric block with interleaved metal electrodes; inner electrodes connected to end terminations; plated tin (NiSn); lead-free.

## Capacitance Range & Thickness (X5R, Representative)
Capacitance range includes 22 nF to 220 µF depending on size and voltage. Thickness classes are indicated by codes BA/CA/.../GD. Non-E6 values available on request.

## Electrical Characteristics (X5R)
- Capacitance tolerance: ±10%, ±20%
- Dissipation factor: depends on voltage/capacitance; typically ≤5%, ≤7%, ≤10%, ≤15%, ≤20%
- Insulation resistance: \(R_{ins}\ge 10\ GΩ\) or \(R_{ins}\times C_r\ge 50/100/500\) s, whichever is less
- Temperature characteristic: \(\Delta C/C \le \pm 15\%\) over -55°C to +85°C
