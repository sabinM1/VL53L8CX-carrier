## Description
The TPS22918-Q1 is a single-channel load switch with both configurable rise time and configurable quick-output discharge. The device contains an N-channel MOSFET that operates over an input voltage range of 1 V to 5.5 V and supports a maximum continuous current of 2 A. The switch is controlled by an on/off input capable of interfacing directly with low-voltage control signals.

The configurable rise time reduces inrush current caused by large bulk load capacitances, reducing or eliminating power-supply droop. The TPS22918-Q1 features a configurable quick output discharge (QOD) pin that controls the fall time of the device to allow design flexibility for power down and sequencing.

The device is available in a small, leaded SOT-23 package (DBV) which allows visual inspection of solder joints. Characterized for operation over -40°C to +105°C free-air temperature range.

## Detailed Description

### Overview
The TPS22918-Q1 is a 5.5-V, 2-A load switch in a 6-pin SOT-23 package. It implements a low-resistance N-channel MOSFET to reduce dropout voltage. The configurable slew rate reduces or eliminates supply droop caused by large inrush currents. The QOD pin allows configuration of the VOUT discharge rate once the switch is disabled. During shutdown, leakage currents are very low. Integrated control logic, driver, charge pump, and output discharge FET reduce solution size and BOM count.

### Functional Block Diagram
- VIN → N-channel MOSFET → VOUT
- Charge pump drives the gate
- Control logic from ON pin
- CT pin sets slew rate
- QOD pin controls output discharge

### Feature Description

#### On and Off Control
The ON pin controls the switch state. ON is active high and has a low threshold, making it compatible with standard GPIO logic thresholds. It can interface with any microcontroller with 1 V or higher GPIO voltage. This pin cannot be left floating and must be driven high or low.

#### Quick Output Discharge (QOD)
The QOD pin can be configured in three ways:
1. **QOD pin shorted to VOUT** — discharge rate controlled by internal \(R_{PD}\).
2. **QOD pin connected to VOUT via external resistor \(R_{EXT}\)** — total QOD resistance:
   \[
   R_{QOD}=R_{PD}+R_{EXT}
   \]
3. **QOD pin left open** — QOD disabled.

#### Adjustable Rise Time (CT)
A capacitor to GND on the CT pin sets the slew rate. The CT capacitor must be rated for 25 V or above.

Approximate relationship:
\[
\text{SR}=0.55\times C_T+30
\]
where SR is slew rate in µs/V and \(C_T\) is capacitance in pF.

Rise time can be calculated by multiplying input voltage by the slew rate.

### Detailed Design Procedure

#### Input Capacitor (\(C_{IN}\))
- A 1-µF ceramic capacitor placed close to VIN and GND is usually sufficient.
- For heavy loads, \(C_{IN}\) about 10× \(C_L\) is recommended to avoid excessive voltage drop.

#### Output Capacitor (\(C_L\)) (Optional)
- \(C_{IN} > C_L\) is highly recommended because of the MOSFET body diode.
- A \(C_L > C_{IN}\) can cause \(V_{OUT}\) to exceed \(V_{IN}\) when the supply is removed, causing current through the body diode from VOUT to VIN.
- A \(C_{IN}\):\(C_L\) ratio of 10:1 is recommended.

#### Shutdown Sequencing During Unexpected System Power Loss
- The QOD function can manage power-down sequencing in the event of unexpected system power loss (e.g., battery removal).
- Determine power-down order; check voltage/timing margins.
- Use QOD table to select \(C_{OUT}\) and \(R_{QOD}\) so fall times match the desired sequence.

Example (4 ms fall time, \(C_L=22\ \mu F\), \(V_{IN}=5\ \text{V}\)):
\[
5.5\ \text{V}=4.5\ \text{V}\times e^{-(4\text{ms})/(R\times 22\mu F)}
\]
\[
R_{QOD}=83.333\ \Omega
\]
With \(R_{PD}\approx 25\ \Omega\) at \(V_{IN}=5\ \text{V}\):
\[
83.333=R_{PD}+R_{EXT}\Rightarrow R_{EXT}=58.333\ \Omega
\]

#### \(V_{IN}\) to VOUT Voltage Drop
\[
\Delta V=I_{LOAD}\times R_{ON}
\]
Choose \(I_{LOAD}\) such that the \(I_{MAX}\) specification is not violated.

#### Inrush Current
\[
I_{INRUSH}=C_L\times\frac{dV_{OUT}}{dt}
\]
Example: \(C_L=22\ \mu F\), \(\Delta V=4\ \text{V}\), \(I_{INRUSH}\le 400\ \text{mA}\):
\[
dt\ge 220\ \mu s
\]
Choose CT yielding rise time > 220 µs. At \(V_{IN}=5\ \text{V}\), \(C_T=220\ \text{pF}\) gives ~650 µs typical rise time:
\[
I_{INRUSH}=22\ \mu F\times 4\ \text{V}/650\ \mu s\approx 135\ \text{mA}
\]

## Power Supply Recommendations
- \(V_{IN}\) range: 1 V to 5.5 V.
- Supply must be well regulated and placed close to the device with the recommended 1-µF bypass capacitor.
- If supply is more than a few inches away, additional bulk capacitance may be required (1 µF electrolytic, tantalum, or ceramic may suffice).

## Layout

### Layout Guidelines
- VIN and VOUT traces should be as short and wide as possible.
- Bypass VIN to GND with low-ESR ceramic capacitor (1 µF X5R/X7R), placed close to pins.
- Bypass VOUT to GND with low-ESR ceramic capacitor, typically one-tenth of the VIN bypass capacitor, placed close to pins.

### Thermal Considerations
- Maximum junction temperature must be ≤150°C.
- Maximum allowable power dissipation:
  \[
  P_{D(MAX)}=\frac{T_{J(MAX)}-T_A}{\theta_{JA}}
  \]
  where \(T_{J(MAX)}=150°C\) and \(\theta_{JA}\) is from the Thermal Information table.
